A little help.

How do I place an order?

Choose a pair and a size, add it to your bag, then prepare an email or WhatsApp request. Send the message in your app. The business confirms availability, delivery and the final total.

Which shoe size should I select?

Choose your sizing system on the product page. Every selection is labelled, for example UK 8. The demonstration conversion table is approximate; confirm the actual product fit before ordering.

View size guide ↗
What about delivery and returns?

Delivery areas, fees and return terms must be confirmed by TEHILLAH before payment. Approved policies will be added before orders open.

Can I pay on this website?

No payment is collected here. TEHILLAH confirms your request directly and provides payment instructions.

How is my information handled?

This storefront stores your shopping bag on this device using browser storage. It does not create accounts or store your name, email or delivery details on a server. When you choose to send an order, your details are shared through your selected email app or WhatsApp, whose privacy terms also apply. Clear your bag to remove the saved items. No analytics or advertising trackers are included.
